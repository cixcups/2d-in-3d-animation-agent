Project workspace for Bang Stick Episode 004, using the repository's Remotion + Three.js 2D-in-3D framework.

## Episode format

- Format: 2D cartoon mystery short
- Platform: vertical Shorts, 9:16
- Resolution: 1080×1920
- Frame rate: 30 fps
- Target duration: approximately 55 seconds (45–60 seconds acceptable)
- Mood: dark, mysterious, cinematic fog, distant city lights

- Use dynamic multi-angle camera coverage and purposeful camera movement for each beat.
- Keep character and important visual information inside the 9:16 safe area.
- Synchronize sound effects with visible actions; keep narration intelligible.
